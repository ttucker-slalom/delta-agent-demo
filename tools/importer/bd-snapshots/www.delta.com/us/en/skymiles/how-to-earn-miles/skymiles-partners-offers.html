Uber x Delta Mileage Earn Terms and Conditions

This offer (“Offer”) is limited to individuals who (a) have enrolled as US-based members of the SkyMiles program and have a US-based Uber account and (b) have linked their SkyMiles account and their Uber account within the Uber App (a “Linked Account”). To earn miles, you must (i) select Delta as your primary loyalty offer partner to earn within your Uber App (“Earning Partner”) and (ii) place a “Qualifying Transaction.” A “Qualifying Transaction” means a completed transaction made using the Uber and/or Uber Eats App, in accordance with one of the Mileage Offers herein, with Delta selected as your Earning Partner for such transaction.  Delta business accounts are not eligible to participate.

Mileage Offer on Qualifying Transactions for Rides:  Linked Account members are eligible to earn miles on their Total Trip Fare (defined below) for the following qualifying rides in the U.S.: (i) 1 mile per $1 USD of Total Ride Fees on Uber X airport rides, (ii) 2 miles per $1 USD of Total Ride Fees on an Uber XL, Uber SUV, Uber Comfort, and Uber Black, and (iii) 3 miles per $1 USD of Total Ride Fees on Uber Reserve Economy and Uber Reserve Premium. “Total Trip Fare” means the total amount spent by the Linked Account in connection with a qualifying Uber ride, excluding any promotions, discounts, taxes, fees, mandatory additional charges, and tips. Uber X airport rides are not eligible to earn miles at airport locations not serviced by Uber. Uber is not available in all locations and some exclusions may apply to ride types (eg. Taxi and Shuttle), or profiles that are associated with Family, Teen, or Guest accounts and are determined in Uber’s sole discretion. If the fare is split between multiple riders, only the fare actually paid by the eligible Linked Account(s) shall be used to calculate a mileage award; Unlinked members splitting a fare will not be eligible to earn.

Mileage Offer on Qualifying Transactions for Orders:  Linked Account members are eligible to earn 1 mile per $1 USD of their total Uber Eats order amount (excluding taxes, fees, service charges, tips, and other non-food/beverage/grocery charges) on Restaurant Delivery and Grocery Delivery orders over $40 USD, in the U.S. Alcohol and similar restricted items (e.g., cannabis) are also not eligible to earn. “Restaurant Delivery” includes any food and restaurant category order placed with a restaurant merchant through Uber Eats. “Grocery Delivery” includes any grocery category order placed with a grocery merchant on Uber Eats from a grocery store through Uber Eats (excludes merchants classified as non-grocery or retail stores such as home improvement retailers, convenience stores, and gas stations).

You may link your Uber account to more than one eligible loyalty offer or program at a time, but you may only select and earn with one Earning Partner at a time.
